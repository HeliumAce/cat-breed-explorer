# Breed traits consistency audit

Reviewed November 7, 2026 against the content checkpoint `f99dcdf`, the 107-entry API inventory, and the source profiles cited in the data. A fresh API fetch confirmed the same 107 IDs and temperament strings.

## Outcome

All 107 catalogue IDs are accounted for: 86 complete trait profiles, 20 partial or unavailable profiles, and the existing Kellas exclusion. Complete profiles include the Chausie alias, leaving 85 distinct complete breed assessments. With the current API image availability, the quiz compares 64 entries; Bambino and Cyprus remain visible in the 66-entry image catalogue but lack sufficient supported ratings for comparison.

There are 30 numerical adjustments across 22 catalogue IDs, plus 187 withdrawn estimates across the 20 entries with evidence limits. Every retained trait still has a rationale. Labels are individually authored alongside the ratings with a source, section and review summary; 86 entries have labels. They replace raw API temperament text in list, search and detail data.

Facts and tips remain at the reviewed content checkpoint. No direct contradiction required a prose change in this pass. A general suggestion to try a puzzle or gentle handling does not establish high intelligence, affection, trainability or compatibility. Physical facts can remain publishable while behavioural ratings remain unavailable.

## Interpretation

The 1–5 values are qualitative editorial assessments, not measured breed statistics, probabilities or registry-published ratings. Three means moderate when evidence supports that assessment; missing evidence is `null`. Intelligence is not a validated cognitive ranking. Child and dog compatibility assume respectful handling and careful introductions, and never follow automatically from a gentle or sociable label.

Grooming reflects effort as well as frequency: light weekly care is lower effort than daily dense-coat or skin/ear maintenance. Seasonal and individual coat differences remain in the rationales. Hair length alone does not establish shedding amount. Vocalization considers conversational frequency and insistence; a soft voice can still be used often. A calm disposition alone does not establish a quiet voice. Reserve with unfamiliar people does not establish poor adaptability.

Coat and colour variants use the same supported behaviour assessment where the sources identify them as the same breed/type. Their grooming and shedding values remain separate when there is supporting evidence. Tests protect British tipped/shorthair/longhair, Manx/Cymric, Exotic coat lengths, Balinese/Javanese, Minuet/Napoleon Longhair, Oriental coat lengths, Singapura/tabby and Chausie aliases.

Useful general or coat-specific tips can still be shown for the other limited profiles, with their editorial basis and sources recorded.
